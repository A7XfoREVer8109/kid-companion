🧠 How to get the answer YOURSELF — the secret every game here teaches

Don't just guess — use the steps! Every game has a 💡 Hint and a 👩‍🏫 Show me how button that walks through the answer step by step, so you can do the next one all on your own.

✖️ Multiplication
  1. Read it as groups: 4 × 3 means 4 groups of 3.
  2. Draw the groups or dots.
  3. Skip-count: 3, 6, 9, 12.
  4. Check with division: 12 ÷ 4 = 3 ✓
➗ Division
  1. It means sharing equally or making equal groups.
  2. Ask the fact-family question: 4 × ? = 12.
  3. Skip-count by 4 until you reach 12 — count your counts.
  4. Check: 4 × 3 = 12 ✓
📖 Reading questions
  1. Read the question first — what is it really asking?
  2. Go back into the story and hunt for clue words.
  3. Point to the sentence that proves your answer.
  4. Say it in your own words.
🐝 Spelling
  1. Say the word slowly, sound by sound.
  2. Clap or count the chunks (syllables).
  3. Spell one chunk at a time.
  4. Write it, cover it, write it again from memory.

🤸 Gymnastics & Cheer Studio!

Learn real gymnastics and cheer moves — one safe step at a time. Watch your companion show you the move in the demo, read each step, then try it slowly. Every great gymnast started with step 1!

⚠️ Safety first — always!
✅ Practice on a soft mat, carpet or grass — never on hard floors, beds or furniture.
✅ Always have a grown-up nearby as your spotter, especially for rolls, bridges and handstands.
✅ Warm up first (see below), wear comfy clothes, tie hair back, and take water breaks.
✅ If anything hurts, feels dizzy, or scares you — STOP and tell a grown-up. No flips or aerial tricks here — those are only for gym classes with a coach.

🧰 My Feelings Toolbox — what to DO with big feelings

Feelings are like weather — they visit, and they pass. You can't stop the weather, but you CAN learn what helps while it's here. Tap a feeling to open its toolbox: real steps kids your age actually use.

    💬 Say this to yourself

    🌋 The #1 rule for ALL big feelings
    1. Stop — freeze like a statue, hands down.
    2. Name it — “I feel angry.” Naming it shrinks it.
    3. Breathe — slow breaths out, like blowing out birthday candles, one by one.
    4. Choose — pick a toolbox step below. Your brain chooses better when it's calm.
    🧊 Cool-down tricks that really work
    1. Run cold water over your wrists, or hold something cold.
    2. Squeeze a pillow or stuffed animal as hard as you can — then let go.
    3. Stomp, shake, or dance the wiggles out for one whole song.
    4. Count backwards from 20, slowly. Race your feelings to calm!
    🗣️ Use your words (the strong way)
    1. Say: “I feel ___ when ___.” Example: “I feel mad when my tower gets knocked down.”
    2. Say what you need: “I need a break” or “Please stop.”
    3. If words won't come, draw it or write it — that counts as using your words too.
