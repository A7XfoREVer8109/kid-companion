✖️ Multiplication
  1. Read it as groups: 4 × 3 means 4 groups of 3.
  2. Draw the groups or dots.
  3. Skip-count: 3, 6, 9, 12.
  4. Check with division: 12 ÷ 4 = 3 ✓
➗ Division
  1. It means sharing equally or making equal groups.
  2. Ask the fact-family question: 4 × ? = 12.
  3. Skip-count by 4 until you reach 12 — count your counts.
  4. Check: 4 × 3 = 12 ✓
📖 Reading questions
  1. Read the question first — what is it really asking?
  2. Go back into the story and hunt for clue words.
  3. Point to the sentence that proves your answer.
  4. Say it in your own words.
🐝 Spelling
  1. Say the word slowly, sound by sound.
  2. Clap or count the chunks (syllables).
  3. Spell one chunk at a time.
  4. Write it, cover it, write it again from memory.

🏀 Basketball Court!

Learn how basketball works, practice one step at a time, and play hoop games. First dribble to first basket — let's go!

⚠️ Court safety — always!
✅ Play on a flat driveway, playground or gym court — watch out for cars, and never chase a runaway ball into the street.
✅ Use a kid-size ball if you can (a Size 5 is made for your age) and a lower hoop (about 8 feet) — scoring is way more fun!
✅ Wear sneakers, tie your laces, take water breaks, and warm up first (see below).
✅ Be a good sport: no pushing, no grabbing — defenders move their feet, not their hands.

📖 How to Play — the whole game in 6 cards

🎯 The goal
  1. Two teams try to throw the ball through the other team's hoop.
  2. Most points when time runs out wins!
  3. In kids' games there are 5 players per team on the court.
🏃 Moving the ball
  1. You must dribble (bounce) the ball while you walk or run.
  2. Stop dribbling and you must pass or shoot — no walking with the ball (that's called traveling!).
  3. Passing to teammates is the fastest way up the court!
🔢 Scoring
  1. A normal basket = 2 points.
  2. A shot from far behind the big arc = 3 points!
  3. A free throw (a free shot after a foul) = 1 point.
🛡️ Defense
  1. Stay between your player and your hoop.
  2. Slide your feet side to side — don't cross them, don't reach in.
  3. Hands up and wide makes you look HUGE.
🤝 Fouls & fairness
  1. No pushing, hitting, holding or tripping — that's a foul.
  2. If you foul a shooter, they get free throws.
  3. Shake hands, help players up, cheer good plays — even the other team's!
⏱️ Game words
  1. Rebound: catching the ball after a missed shot.
  2. Assist: a pass that leads straight to a basket — passing is a superpower!
  3. Fast break: racing up the court quickly after getting the ball.

🔥 Warm-up first (2 minutes!)

1. Jog around the court — 1 lap  ·  2. 20 jumping jacks  ·  3. Arm circles + finger flicks (wake up those dribbling fingers!)  ·  4. Side-to-side slides like a crab 🦀  ·  5. 10 easy bounces with each hand

🏀 How to Dribble — Jr. NBA Fundamentals

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Stance first: knees bent, chest up, like sitting in an invisible chair.
2️⃣ Push the ball down with your finger pads — never slap it with your palm.
3️⃣ Keep the bounce beside your hip, no higher than your waist.
4️⃣ Eyes UP, not on the ball — try spotting a color across the court while you dribble.
5️⃣ Practice right hand, left hand, then switch on a clap!

⭐ Try it after: 10 controlled bounces each hand without the ball escaping. Chased it? That's normal — reset and go again!

🔀 Crossover Dribble — Kids Learning Together

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Start low with the ball in one hand, bouncing between your feet.
2️⃣ Push the ball across your body, low and quick, to the other hand.
3️⃣ Catch it with the other hand and bounce it right back — chant “bounce left, bounce right!”
4️⃣ Keep it below your knees: low ball = safe ball.
5️⃣ Slow first! Speed only comes after 20 tidy crossovers.

⭐ Try it after: Crossover back and forth 10 times standing still, like the ball is on a tiny train track.

🦵 Between the Legs — Youth Drill

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Master normal dribbling and crossovers FIRST — this is a bonus-level move!
2️⃣ Step one foot forward to make a “tunnel” between your legs.
3️⃣ Bounce the ball through the tunnel, low, from one hand to the other.
4️⃣ Start standing still and SLOW — walking comes much later.
5️⃣ Lost the ball? Laugh, fetch it, reset your tunnel. Even pros drop it learning this!

⭐ Try it after: Just 3 in a row standing still is a superstar score at your age!

🛡️ Defensive Stance — Quick Stance Drill

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Feet WIDE — wider than your shoulders, like a crab. 🦀
2️⃣ Knees bent, bottom back — sit in the invisible chair.
3️⃣ Chest up, back straight, on the fronts of your feet, ready to spring.
4️⃣ Hands wide and active — but defense is played with your FEET, not by reaching.
5️⃣ Hold your stance for 5–10 seconds without standing up tall. Feel the leg burn? That's defense muscles growing!

⭐ Try it after: On “GO!” drop into your stance and freeze. Can you hold 10 seconds?

🦵 Between the Legs — Howcast Slow Breakdown

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Stay LOW the whole time — the ball never rises above your waist.
2️⃣ Ball in your right hand? Step forward with your LEFT leg, and bounce it through to your left hand.
3️⃣ Your body protects the ball as it changes direction.
4️⃣ Try the standing scissor drill: through the legs, switch feet, through again.
5️⃣ Head up, fingertip control — watch how the coach changes direction right after the move.

⭐ Try it after: The scissor drill — 5 slow ones each way, standing in one spot.

⚽ Soccer Field!

Learn how soccer works, practice one step at a time, and play field games. First touch to first goal — let's play!

⚠️ Field safety — always!
✅ Play on grass or a proper field, away from roads — never chase a runaway ball into the street.
✅ Use a kid-size ball if you can (a Size 3 or 4 is made for your age) and wear sneakers or cleats with laces tied.
✅ No slide tackles and no kicking near anyone's head — defenders win the ball with their feet in front, safely.
✅ Warm up first (see below), take water breaks, and be a good sport: no pushing, no tripping, help players up.

📖 How to Play — the whole game in 6 cards

🎯 The goal
  1. Two teams try to kick the ball into the other team's goal.
  2. Each goal = 1 point. Most goals when time runs out wins!
  3. Kids' teams are smaller (like 7 against 7) so everyone touches the ball lots.
🦶 Feet only!
  1. Only the goalkeeper may use hands — and only inside their big box.
  2. Everyone else uses feet, legs, chest and head.
  3. Touch it with your hand or arm? That's a handball — the other team gets the ball.
🏃 Moving the ball
  1. Dribble with lots of little touches to keep the ball close.
  2. Pass to teammates — the ball moves faster than any runner!
  3. After you pass, MOVE into space so you can get it back.
🛡️ Defense
  1. Stay between your player and your goal — that's called goal side.
  2. Stand sideways-on like a surfer 🏄, knees bent, and be patient.
  3. Don't dive in! Slow them down, steer them to the sideline, wait for a wobbly touch.
🔄 Restarts
  1. Ball goes out on the side? Throw-in — two hands, from behind your head, feet on the ground.
  2. Out behind the goal by the defenders? Corner kick for the attackers!
  3. After a goal, the team that was scored on kicks off from the center.
⏱️ Game words
  1. Assist: the pass right before a goal.
  2. Clean sheet: a goalkeeper's team lets in ZERO goals!
  3. Hat-trick: one player scores 3 goals in a game. 🎩

🔥 Warm-up first (2 minutes!)

1. Jog around the field — 1 lap  ·  2. 20 toe taps on top of the ball (light and quick!)  ·  3. Side-to-side shuffles like a goalkeeper  ·  4. Big leg swings, forwards and sideways  ·  5. Gentle passes with a partner or a wall

⚽ Dribbling Basics — Beginner Drills for U7–U10

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ “In and outs”: one touch with the inside of your foot, one with the outside — with a step in between.
2️⃣ Two touches inside, two touches outside — feel how your foot wraps around the ball.
3️⃣ Then one touch right foot, one touch left foot, all the way down the line.
4️⃣ Take extra steps between touches so you're always on the right side of the ball.
5️⃣ Quality first, speed later — tidy touches beat fast, messy ones.

⭐ Try it after: In-and-outs down a line of shoes and back, nice and slow, both feet.

🚧 Cone Dribbling — Obstacle Course Control

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Set up a wiggly course of cones (or shoes, or stuffed animals!).
2️⃣ Dribble around each one with lots of LITTLE touches — the ball never gets far from your feet.
3️⃣ Use inside, outside, inside, outside as you weave.
4️⃣ Look UP between touches to see the next cone coming.
5️⃣ Finish the course, then race your own best time — control first, speed second!

⭐ Try it after: Build a 5-cone slalom in the yard and weave through it 3 times without touching a cone.

🦶 Passing — One-Touch Passing for Beginners

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Pass with the INSIDE of your foot — the big flat part is your steering wheel.
2️⃣ Plant your other foot beside the ball, pointing where the pass should go.
3️⃣ Lock your ankle (stiff foot!) and swing through the middle of the ball.
4️⃣ Keep it on the ground, rolling smoothly to your partner's feet.
5️⃣ Ready to level up? Try one-touch: pass it straight back without stopping it first!

⭐ Try it after: 10 passes back and forth with a partner or a wall — count how many land right at their feet.

🎯 Shooting Technique — Strike With Your Laces

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Plant foot beside the ball, pointing at the goal.
2️⃣ Strike through the middle of the ball with your LACES (the bony top of your foot) — not your toes!
3️⃣ Lock your ankle and point your toes down as you swing.
4️⃣ Lean slightly OVER the ball — leaning back sends it to the moon. 🌙
5️⃣ Aim for the corners, low — the hardest places for a goalkeeper to reach.

⭐ Try it after: 5 shots at a target in the corner of the goal (a cone or a backpack works!).

💥 How to Kick With Power — Epic Soccer Training

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Power comes from TECHNIQUE, not just big muscles!
2️⃣ Strike the ball at the inside base of your foot/ankle, where your foot locks in strong.
3️⃣ Kicking with the very end of your toes is the #1 kid mistake — it makes weak, wobbly shots.
4️⃣ Drive all the way THROUGH the ball — your leg keeps swinging after contact.
5️⃣ Practice with BOTH feet: right-foot power, then left-foot power.

⭐ Try it after: Kick at a wall target (with a grown-up's okay!) and feel the difference between toe-kicks and locked-ankle kicks.

🛡️ Defending — The Patient Jockey (1v1)

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Rule #1 of defending: DON'T DIVE IN. Lunging = getting dribbled past.
2️⃣ Get into the “surfboard stance”: side-on, knees bent, low and balanced. 🏄
3️⃣ Shuffle backwards (jockeying), staying about an arm's length away, never crossing your feet.
4️⃣ Steer the attacker toward the SIDELINE — the sideline is your extra defender friend!
5️⃣ Wait for their heavy, wobbly touch… THEN step in and poke the ball away.

⭐ Try it after: The Shadow Game — mirror a partner's slow jog side to side without touching them, staying in your surfboard stance.

🔄 Forward Rolls — Tumbling Fun for Kids

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Start in a crawl/tuck position, hands down on the mat.
2️⃣ Tuck your head right into your chest — look at your belly button!
3️⃣ Push your bottom up and over, rolling over your ROUND back (never the top of your head).
4️⃣ A partner or grown-up can help keep your chin tucked at first.
5️⃣ Finish on your feet — repeat 5 times, a little smoother each roll.

⭐ Try it after: 3 slow rolls on a mat with your spotter beside you.

⭐ Cartwheels — Sideways Star Power

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Always start with a LUNGE: strong leg in front and bent, arms straight up by your ears.
2️⃣ Travel sideways in a straight line, like a star spinning along a line.
3️⃣ Hands land one at a time, shoulder-width apart: hand, hand…
4️⃣ Legs pass over your body one at a time: …foot, foot!
5️⃣ Land back in a lunge, facing the way you started. Ta-da!

⭐ Try it after: Just the hand-hand-foot-foot rhythm, standing still and tapping it out.

🧱 Handstands — Learn Safely, Step by Step

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Warm up and stretch your neck, back and wrists FIRST.
2️⃣ Practice against a WALL with a grown-up spotter the whole time.
3️⃣ Hands flat, fingers spread wide like starfish — that's your balance base.
4️⃣ Walk your feet up the wall slowly instead of kicking up.
5️⃣ Only hold a few seconds, come down slowly, and rest — dizziness means STOP.

⭐ Try it after: Wall walks only — feet up two steps, hold 3 counts, walk down.

🌉 Backbend & Bridge — Strong, Stretchy Backs

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat and close to your bottom.
2️⃣ Hands by your ears, fingers pointing toward your shoulders.
3️⃣ Push through hands AND feet together, lifting your tummy to the sky.
4️⃣ Hold only 3 slow counts with your spotter right beside you.
5️⃣ Lower down SLOWLY like a deflating balloon — never just drop!

⭐ Try it after: One bridge, 3 counts, slow lower. Rest, breathe, smile.

🙌 Basic Cheer Motions — High V, Low V, T & Touchdown

Step by step — what to watch for:

1️⃣ Hands in FISTS, thumbs on the outside, wrists flat — always.
2️⃣ High V: straight arms up into a V, slightly in front of your body, palms forward.
3️⃣ Low V: the same V pointing down (pretend you're carrying two bags of groceries!).
4️⃣ Diagonals: one arm High V, one arm Low V — then switch!
5️⃣ T Motion: arms straight out like a letter T. Touchdown: both arms straight UP beside your ears!

⭐ Try it after: Learn the motions WITHOUT pom-poms first — add them once your arms know the way.

🌟 Basic Cheer Jumps — Pencil, Tuck & Spread Eagle

Step by step — the counts (5, 6, 7, 8!):

1️⃣ On 1: hit a High V. Hold on 2.
2️⃣ On 3: swing your arms down, hands crossing slightly, knees bent — this is your spring loading!
3️⃣ On 4: JUMP! Pencil = straight body, T position arms. Tuck = knees up to your chest. Spread Eagle = arms and legs make an X!
4️⃣ On 5: land with knees bent and chest up, arms by your sides.
5️⃣ Hold 6, stand tall on 7. Every jump starts and lands the SAME way — only the middle changes!

⭐ Try it after: Pencil jumps to counts, 3 in a row, landing soft and frozen-still each time.

🦵 The Toe Touch — Cheer's Most Famous Jump

Step by step — the counts:

1️⃣ On 1: hit a High V. Hold on 2.
2️⃣ On 3: swing arms in, legs bent, ready to spring.
3️⃣ On 4: JUMP into a T motion and bring your legs UP to your hands — your hands don't reach down, your legs come UP! Point those toes!
4️⃣ Keep your chest UP in the air — no folding forwards.
5️⃣ On 5: snap your feet back together and land knees-bent; hold 6, stand on 7.

⭐ Try it after: This is a stretch goal — flexibility through your legs and back helps most. Practice the counts with a plain T jump first!

🧰 My Feelings Toolbox — what to DO with big feelings

Feelings are like weather — they visit, and they pass. You can't stop the weather, but you CAN learn what helps while it's here. Tap a feeling to open its toolbox: real steps kids your age actually use.

    💬 Say this to yourself

    🌋 The #1 rule for ALL big feelings
    1. Stop — freeze like a statue, hands down.
    2. Name it — “I feel angry.” Naming it shrinks it.
    3. Breathe — slow breaths out, like blowing out birthday candles, one by one.
    4. Choose — pick a toolbox step below. Your brain chooses better when it's calm.
    🧊 Cool-down tricks that really work
    1. Run cold water over your wrists, or hold something cold.
    2. Squeeze a pillow or stuffed animal as hard as you can — then let go.
    3. Stomp, shake, or dance the wiggles out for one whole song.
    4. Count backwards from 20, slowly. Race your feelings to calm!
    🗣️ Use your words (the strong way)
    1. Say: “I feel ___ when ___.” Example: “I feel mad when my tower gets knocked down.”
    2. Say what you need: “I need a break” or “Please stop.”
    3. If words won't come, draw it or write it — that counts as using your words too.
