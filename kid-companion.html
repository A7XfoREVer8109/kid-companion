Meet your Kid Companion ✨

A friendly buddy you can name, dress up, chat with, and ask for homework and game help. Now with a Reading, Math & Spelling Assistant in the Learn tab — short stories with questions, step-by-step math practice, and listen-and-spell word games. It remembers you — right here on this device — so every visit picks up where you left off.

For grown-ups: This is a safe, scripted play companion — not a live AI and it never chats with strangers. Chats, names, favorites, and learning scores are saved only in this browser on this device (nothing is sent to a server). The Read Aloud / Listen buttons use your device's built-in voice. Search links open Google with SafeSearch locked on. Clear everything anytime in the My Memory tab. If browser data is cleared, the memory resets.
